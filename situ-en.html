WHEN YOU ARE STUCK

Situation GuidesNot organised by cancer type — organised by where you are stuck: just diagnosed, being the family, treatment stalling, wanting more, wanting to stop.

The cancer guides answer a question about your disease. These answer a different kind of question: what to do in the month when the reports are not back yet, who takes leave and who keeps working, whether a recurrence really means changing drugs, what those names outside standard treatment actually are, and the sentence a lot of people never say out loud — would it be easier to stop. Thirty-five articles, none of them specific to one cancer, one question per article, with the primary sources linked. Where the evidence is weak I say so, and where I could not find something I say I could not find it — including for the treatment I perform myself.

3When Treatment Stalls

After a bad report the three easiest mistakes are: drawing a conclusion from one scan, skipping confirmation because you are in a hurry, and paying for a test that will not change the decision. These eight put confirming before acting.

It came back: the first move is not a new drug
39 studies of paired specimens: a metastasis may not carry the receptors the original tumour did. What re-biopsy changes, what cannot wait.

5Is Letting Go Easier
