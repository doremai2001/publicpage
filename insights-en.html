Medical Insights

Medical insightsTreatment guidelines are rewritten by new trials every year or two. These are my read-throughs: what a study said, what it did not say, and whether it means anything for patients in Taiwan right now.

Every piece links to the primary literature. When a number looks compelling, check three things alongside it: which patients the trial enrolled, how much toxicity increased, and whether it is available in Taiwan today.
