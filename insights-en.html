Medical Insights

Medical insightsTreatment guidelines are rewritten by new trials every year or two. These are my read-throughs: what a study said, what it did not say, and whether it means anything for patients in Taiwan right now.

Every piece links to the primary literature. When a number looks compelling, check three things alongside it: which patients the trial enrolled, how much toxicity increased, and whether it is available in Taiwan today.

Radiation Therapy

Is proton therapy actually better?
Two phase 3 oropharyngeal trials in The Lancet reached different conclusions — clinician-scored toxicity fell, patient-reported outcomes did not move.
RADIATION THERAPY · 5 references
Five fractions or twenty-five?
Two pre-operative schedules for rectal cancer. Does a fivefold difference in fractions mean a difference in outcome? The answer shifted again in the past two years.
RADIATION THERAPY · 4 references

Lung Cancer

After chemotherapy ends, what else is there?
Maintenance in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer is usually short. The IMforte trial addressed the question directly.
LUNG CANCER · 6 references
Can adding a drug up front push resistance further away?
Survival data are now out for both FLAURA2 and MARIPOSA. Is the extra time worth the extra toxicity?
LUNG CANCER · 5 references
When the targeted drug stops working, which road do you take?
Tumours have more than one bypass route, and MET is a common one. The SAFFRON trial reported topline results — but the full data are not out.
LUNG CANCER · 6 references
A failed trial that proved the current standard is good enough
SKYSCRAPER-03 tried to beat the current standard with two immunotherapy agents. It came out even. "A few months longer" is not the whole story.
LUNG CANCER · 4 references

Head & Neck

Can a blood test decide your treatment?
EBV DNA in nasopharyngeal carcinoma tracks the tumour. The EP-STAR trial tried using it the other way round — to set treatment intensity.
HEAD & NECK · 4 references

Breast & Gynaecologic

Is hormone-positive breast cancer really over at five years?
Recurrence in this subtype does not stop at year five. CDK4/6 inhibitors address exactly that window — but the absolute numbers need reading alongside.
BREAST & GYN · 3 references
Three in ten endometrial cancers may not need chemotherapy first
It depends on whether the tumour is dMMR. KEYNOTE-C93 reported topline results: direction clear, magnitude unknown.
BREAST & GYN · 4 references
Immunotherapy in ovarian cancer finally has a result
Immunotherapy has underperformed in ovarian cancer for years. KEYNOTE-B96 combined it with chemotherapy and restricted it to PD-L1-positive disease — a real but modest gain.
BREAST & GYN · 7 references

Urologic & GI

A thirty-year-old pathway just changed
Peri-operative treatment for muscle-invasive bladder cancer has a new regimen. Efficacy improved; discontinuation rose from 11% to 35%.
UROLOGIC & GI · 5 references
One more drug around surgery — is it worth it?
In high-risk localised prostate cancer, PROTEUS gained under five percentage points in five-year metastasis-free survival — but the downstream endpoints separated further.
UROLOGIC & GI · 4 references
RAS was undruggable. Something finally got through.
Over 90% of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinomas carry a RAS mutation, long considered undruggable. RASolute 302 loosened that assumption.
UROLOGIC & GI · 5 references

Vaccines & Prevention

An entire age band, zero deaths
In England between 2020 and 2024, not one woman aged 20 to 24 died of cervical cancer. The first mortality data for HPV vaccination.
VACCINE & PREVENTION · 4 references
A made-to-measure cancer vaccine reaches phase 3
The word "vaccine" is misleading — this is not prevention but a post-operative treatment manufactured for one patient. INTerpath-001 met its endpoints, but no numbers have been released.
VACCINE & PREVENTION · 6 references
