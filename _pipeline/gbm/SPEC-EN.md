> <p>My position first: the radiotherapy part of glioblastoma treatment is the part I do myself; the other side of every comparison in this topic is my neurosurgical colleagues' operation and my neuro-oncology colleagues' drugs. ## 2. The passages English will break

- **紅線 1 (`gb-numbers`)**, bidirectional and the highest in the series. Median survival is
  「一半的人在這條線之前、一半在之後」 → "half the people are before this line and half after it",
  never "the average patient lives X months" and never anything readable as a personal deadline.
  The tail's three numbers each keep their own denominator (trial cohort five-year 9.8%, n=287;
  real-world 4%, 1,291 of 28,748; ten-year about 0.71%, 162 cases under the old classification) —
  **never pick the highest one**. **The word "cure" does not appear**, in any form: the Chinese was
  deliberately rewritten to 「治不好的癌症病人」 → "people whose cancer cannot be put right", not
  "incurable". The author's own position — 「我不給你一個數字當期限，因為那個數字不是為你一個人
  算的」 → "I am not giving you a number to treat as a deadline, because that number was not
  calculated for you alone" — survives as a full sentence.
- **修正 4 (`gb-mgmt`)**, the second most dangerous passage. OS 11.8 vs 12.7 "no difference could be
  seen" and PFS 5.9 vs 10.3 "there is a difference" are two separate statements and stay separate;
  「從來沒有人對這個族群做過前瞻隨機比較」 → "no one has ever compared this prospectively and at
  random in that group"; Perry's 10.0 vs 7.9 keeps **P=0.055** and the "just failed to cross the
  line" gloss. **Nothing readable as "unmethylated means you don't need it".** The instruction not
  to stop the drug on your own survives as an imperative sentence.
- **修正 3 (`gb-standard`, `gb-newthings`)**: NRG-BN001 has read out — photon dose escalation
  negative (hazard ratio 0.95, P=0.25); the proton centres' hazard ratio 0.81, P=0.11 with
  "by protocol, protons were never compared with photons" and "the proton centres' **control** arm
  was also better (22.0 vs 16.3 months) — that is a centre effect, not a beam effect". Both
  articles must say this the same way.
- **紅線 7 / 修正 6 (`gb-newthings`)**: 「三個第三期，主要終點沒有一個達到」 and
  **CheckMate-498 was significantly worse (hazard ratio 1.31, P=.0037)** — "worse", not "did not
  help". DCVax-L keeps all four design problems. REGOMA is only "later not confirmed"
  (GBM AGILE: not superior, more toxicity). **The word "worth" never appears.**
- **修正 8 / 紅線 8 (`gb-followup`)**: use Blakstad's denominator — of the 166 whose scans looked
  worse, 53 were pseudoprogression and 113 were real progression, about a third. **Never "single
  digits"** (that is the immunotherapy figure on the live page). The RANO 2.0 twelve-week rule is
  quoted as written, with 「不是你可以自己領走的等待期」 → "not a waiting period you can award
  yourself" — read `sit-pseudo-en.html` and match its register, never looser.
- **修正 12 (`gb-warning-signs`)**: the VTE passage is "recognise the symptoms, come back the same
  day". **ITAC's grades and "not recommended for routine prophylaxis" are not written for patients.**
  The odds ratio keeps "an odds ratio is not a multiple of the probability". PRODIGE's
  non-significant bleeding (hazard ratio 4.2, P=0.22) stays alongside.
- **Fever rule**: "a single reading of 38.0°C — go; don't take it again, don't wait for it to
  persist, don't wait for 38.3" — read `care-fever-en.html` and match its wording exactly.
- **Steroids (`gb-steroid`)**: no mg figure is ever presented as advice; the only mg that appears is
  labelled as a study's grouping. 「減量表沒有一份是為你算的」 → "no tapering schedule was
  calculated for you".
- **Reimbursement register**: 「查不到…問醫務課／個管師」 → "I could not find a listed item — ask
  the medical affairs office or your case manager"; never "not covered" as a flat claim. NHI clause
  quotations are marked as the translator's rendering of the clause. **The temozolomide clause
  (9.25) contains no "six months"** — it is prior approval every three months with imaging.
- **No in-house configuration anywhere**: no machine names, no self-pay amounts, no "our hospital
  has / doesn't have", no named hospitals. Availability is always "not every hospital has it — ask
  your doctor where you would be referred".
- **Word-proportion rule**: 「約三分之一」= "about a third", 「一成」= "10%" — every number
  identical, hedges at equal strength.
