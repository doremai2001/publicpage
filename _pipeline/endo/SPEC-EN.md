## 2. The passages English will break

- **紅線 1 (em-fertility)**, the highest-risk article. Nothing readable as "you can just take the
  drug instead of having surgery"; nothing readable as "a response means it is cured". The three
  outcome figures stay in one paragraph — **complete response about 79.7%, recurrence 35.3%,
  live birth 20.5%** — each with its denominator. The guideline's self-assessment is mandatory:
  **no recommendation reaches Level I, only 16 of 48 reach Level II, 66% rest on observational
  data, professional experience and consensus** (quote the English original, it already is English).
  「完成生育後建議手術」 → quote the guideline verbatim ("Completion surgery is recommended after
  completing childbearing", with its "due to a high recurrence rate"). The reverse reading —
  "if you want children, forget it" — is equally forbidden.
- **紅線 2 (em-adjuvant)**, bidirectional. PORTEC-1's three conclusions stay in one frame:
  5-year locoregional recurrence 4% vs 14%; 5-year overall survival 81% vs 85% (p=0.31, the paper's
  own conclusion is "no effect on overall survival"); treatment-related complications 25% vs 6%.
  **Nothing readable as "irradiation is the safer bet"; nothing readable as "it does not prolong
  survival so it can be skipped".** The GOG-99 high-intermediate-risk definition must NOT become a
  tick-list the reader can score herself on — 「不是逐項打勾就算得出來的」 → "it is not something you
  can arrive at by ticking boxes".
- **紅線 5 / 修正 2 (em-molecular-adjuvant)**: PORTEC-4a's three qualifiers stay in **one
  paragraph** — (a) non-inferiority, 「證明的是『沒差太多』，不是『一樣好』」 → "proves 'not much
  worse', not 'as good as'"; (b) the favourable subgroup's vaginal recurrence 4.1% vs 0.9%
  (HR 3.97, 95% CI 0.48–32.95), point estimate fourfold, small denominator; (c) **favourable is not
  the same as POLEmut**. MMRd keeps all three labels (direction unfavourable, not significant,
  small n) and must never read as "chemotherapy helps here too". p53abn keeps "post-hoc analysis".
  RAINBO is "ongoing", **2030 or thereabouts, never 2028**.
- **紅線 6 (em-immuno)**: every efficacy figure carries its MMR status and n. DUO-E's subgroup
  numbers could not be found — say so, do not invent them. KEYNOTE-B21 (overall HR 1.02) stays as
  the counter-example: "effective in advanced or recurrent disease" is not "effective added after
  surgery".
- **紅線 3 (em-bleeding)**: the ultrasound threshold is "a reference for deciding whether to take
  tissue today", never "a pass to go home and watch". ACOG's ">99% negative predictive value at
  ≤4 mm" is quoted **only alongside** Clarke 2020 (5.8% vs 3.6%) and Doll 2024 (11.4% false-negative
  probability, 12.4% in the postmenopausal-bleeding subgroup). **No causal sentence of the
  "leaving it will make it advanced" kind** — quote FIGO's own "is controversial" instead.
- **紅線 4 (em-staging-2023)**: 「分期改了，治療沒有自動跟著改」 → the change is to the language of
  description; the randomised evidence behind adjuvant treatment was **recruited under FIGO 2009
  and 1988**. BGCS and BAGP recommending that 2009 continue to be used is quoted verbatim.
  Taiwan's adoption is a gap — never state which version Taiwan uses.
- **紅線 7 (em-lynch)**: the three guideline conflicts stay side by side, each in the guideline's own
  words (two-antibody vs four-antibody; ESGO's annual ultrasound and biopsy vs Manchester's "does
  not recommend invasive gynaecological surveillance"; PMS2's three contradictory sentences,
  including the patient representatives' view). "No family history so no need to test" must be
  impossible to read.
- **紅線 8 (em-obesity)**: **no sentence may read as blame in English either.** Incidence evidence
  and recurrence/survival evidence are two different strengths and stay separated; "losing weight
  will stop it coming back" is written as "I could not find evidence for that". The SGO/SAGES
  quotation keeps its "in this population" qualifier.
- **紅線 9 (em-recurrence)**: both directions. GOG-0238's 3-year progression-free survival 73% keeps
  its full cohort label and must not be extrapolated to pelvic, distant, previously irradiated or
  non-endometrioid disease; the 2% denominator stays. PORTEC-1 is labelled "1990s, without
  image-guided brachytherapy, historical comparison only".
- **紅線 10 / 修正 12 (em-hormone-after)**: never "the guideline says you may / may not". ESGO 2025
  has no such recommendation — say so. GOG-137 keeps all three labels (stopped early, 41%
  adherence, no data above FIGO stage II); Cochrane's "very low certainty" is quoted. The Taiwanese
  label's contraindication and EMAS/IGCS's "may consider" are **placed side by side and not
  reconciled**, and the English must not explain for the reader whether "known or suspected"
  covers a disease-free survivor.
- **修正 7 (em-followup)**: ESGO 2025's four follow-up recommendations are all **V, A** and include
  "there is no evidence that follow-up visits improve overall survival" — quote it. SEOM-GEICO's
  intervals carry their **V, C**. The "not recommended" for serum biomarkers and vaginal cytology is
  **[I, A]** — a strong recommendation *against*, never diluted into "the evidence is weak, ask your
  doctor".
- **修正 9 (em-lymphedema)**: the first section is still "the number depends on who measured it and
  how". 1.3%–49.4% with each figure's ascertainment method attached. Wedin 2021's sentence is quoted
  as it stands in English. **Odds ratios are not multiples of probability** — say so.
- **Reimbursement register**: 「查不到列項，問醫務課／個管師」 → "I could not find a listed item —
  ask the medical affairs office or your case manager"; **never "not covered" as a flat claim, and
  never "so you will have to pay for it yourself"**. NHI clause quotes: translate faithfully and mark
  them as translations of the clause. Zero-hit full-text searches are reported as zero hits.
- **Institution names**: keep them exactly where the Chinese has them (study source / official
  price page); add none; carry no evaluative adjective.
- **Word-proportion rule**: 「約三分之二」= "about two-thirds", 「一成」= "10%", 「九成」=
  "nine in ten" — every number identical; hedges at equal strength.
