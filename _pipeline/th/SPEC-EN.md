## 1. 固定利益揭露（逐字，只出現在 `th-ebrt-en.html` 的第一個 `<p>`）

> My position first: external beam radiotherapy is the part of this I do myself, and the conclusion of this article is that in differentiated thyroid cancer most people do not need it. That conclusion does me no good. I am writing it here anyway, because it is what the present evidence supports. Below I set out what the few situations that genuinely call for it look like, and who has to decide together in those situations — the people judging whether to go this far should not be radiation oncologists alone.

## 2. 名詞對照（逐字固定，全專題一致）

- 微小乳突癌 → papillary microcarcinoma／主動監測 → active surveillance／單葉切除 → lobectomy／全甲狀腺切除 → total thyroidectomy／補全切 → completion thyroidectomy／中央區廓清 → central neck dissection／預防性 → prophylactic
- 副甲狀腺低下 → hypoparathyroidism／喉返神經 → recurrent laryngeal nerve／聲帶麻痺 → vocal cord palsy
- 放射碘 → radioiodine（第一次出現寫 radioiodine (I-131)）／殘餘消融 → remnant ablation／低碘飲食 → low-iodine diet／停藥 → thyroid hormone withdrawal／rhTSH → recombinant human TSH
