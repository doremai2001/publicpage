## 2. The two dividing lines, which the English breaks more easily than the Chinese

1. **Non-muscle-invasive (Ta / T1 / CIS) against muscle-invasive (T2 and above).** English has
   the ready phrase "superficial bladder cancer", and EAU explicitly says **do not use it**. Never
   write it. Every figure carries its side of this line; a survival number without a side is a
   defect even when the arithmetic is right.
2. **Bladder against upper tract (renal pelvis and ureter).** Same histology, different surgery,
   different follow-up, completely different Taiwanese epidemiology. Numbers do not cross.

Three more labels that get dropped in translation and must not be: **CIS present or not**,
**hydronephrosis present or not**, and **whether the first resection was complete and contained
detrusor muscle**. Those three are the core of trimodality selection and the reason some cohorts
look so good.

## 3. The passages English will break

- **紅線 1 / 修正 1, 2, 11, 12, 13 (`bl-two-roads`)** — the topic's highest risk, bidirectional.
  Neither "preservation is second-best" nor "preservation is just as good".
  **修正 1's honest sentence has three parts, in this order, none omitted**: quote EAU 2026's own
  line — "**No successfully completed RCTs are available comparing the outcomes of TMT with RC**" —
  then **SPARE**, closed for accrual with 45 patients (2007-07-20 to 2010-02-12), then **Sell 1991
  (DAVECA 8201, n=183, T2–T4a)**, which did complete but compared preoperative radiotherapy plus
  cystectomy against radical radiotherapy plus salvage, in the pre-cisplatin era — not today's
  trimodality therapy.
  **Zlotta 2023 is propensity-score-matched throughout** (74% vs 74%, 83% vs 85% p=0.057, 76% vs
  76%) — the IPTW figures are not to be mixed in, and the matching method is named in the sentence.
  **Selection bias runs in three directions and all three are written**: matched cohorts favour
  TMT; NCDB/SEER favour radical cystectomy; **Robesti 2025 found informative censoring favouring
  cystectomy and cystectomy was still superior after correction** — that third one is the proof
  the article is honest, and it must survive translation intact.
  **Salvage cystectomy ~19.2%**, with salvage complications 67–72% and a third of patients still
  scoring worse on bladder function at five years.
- **修正 2 (`bl-tmt`, `bl-two-roads`)** — the author's declared position, which the English must
  not inflate. It rests on **three pillars in this order**: ① what the guidelines say (EAU "For all
  suitable candidates, both bladder-preserving techniques and RC should be discussed" and "should
  be offered to all suitable candidates … and not only to patients with significant comorbidities";
  NICE "Offer a choice of radical cystectomy or radiotherapy with a radiosensitiser"); ② Taiwan's
  NHI pays for every component of both roads; ③ the author's own clinic observation, marked as his
  view, placed after the disclosure. **Forbidden in English as in Chinese**: "Taiwan is below the
  international rate"; any Taiwanese utilisation percentage (there is none); any attribution
  sentence about who refers or does not. **Mandatory**: the honest addendum that internationally
  the proportion is rising — SEER 2010–2022 for T2N0M0 went 11.9% → 26.2%, against EAU's own
  "approximately 25-30% of cystectomy candidates may be appropriate".
- **修正 11 (`bl-tmt`)** — BC2001's three numbers as the paper reports them: locoregional
  disease-free survival; overall survival **not significant**; and grade 3–4 adverse events
  **36.0% vs 27.5%, p=0.07 — not significant** during treatment, and **8.3% vs 15.7%, p=0.07,
  lower in the chemoradiotherapy arm** during follow-up. Quote the authors' own direction.
- **紅線 6 / 甲1 (`bl-cystectomy`)** — the defect the safety review caught, and the one the English
  is most likely to reintroduce. EAU's figure is that **radical cystectomy alone gives about half
  of patients five-year survival** — it is the outcome **after** the operation and the reason
  neoadjuvant chemotherapy exists. **There is no figure anywhere for five-year survival without
  surgery, and no sentence may imply one.** The costs (90-day mortality 2.5–8% by cohort type,
  complications 58.5%, readmission ~25%) sit beside **why this is the standard treatment**, not
  beside a comparison group that does not exist. No diversion is written as superior; EAU's own
  "no RCTs compare diversions, not possible to recommend one" is the authority.
- **紅線 4 / 修正 8, 9, 10 (`bl-bcg`, `bl-bcg-fail`)** — BCG is live attenuated mycobacteria
  instilled into the bladder: **not chemotherapy and not a vaccination**. The TICE label's own
  line ("not a vaccine for the prevention of cancer") and its systemic-infection triggers are
  already English — **quote them, do not re-translate them**. The same-day-return thresholds are
  concrete and stay concrete. The **BCG-unresponsive definition** is quoted from the **FDA August
  2024 revised draft guidance**, labelled as such, with its **adequate BCG** requirement. On the
  shortage, the **only citable current status is FDA CBER's page updated 2026-04-10**; EAU's
  linked statement is a 2012 scanned PDF and AUA's page was last updated September 2020, and
  **neither may be presented as current**; Taiwan has no official statement and that is a gap.
  Dose reduction gets its evidence in **both directions** (EORTC 30962, NIMBUS) and **nothing may
  read as "so your doctor short-changed you"**. INLEXZO's label line — "**delaying cystectomy… can
  be lethal**" — is mandatory in `bl-bcg-fail` and is already English.
- **紅線 5 / 修正 7 (`bl-nmibc-risk`)** — "the EAU 2021 model replaced the EORTC 2006 tables" is
  **wrong and unwritable**. EAU 2026 rates both Strong; the 2021 model predicts **progression
  only** and was built on **BCG-naïve** patients (n=3,401), and EAU records that it **overestimates
  once BCG is given**: very-high risk five-year progression **40% untreated against 14.9–22% after
  adequate BCG**, with a Japanese validation C-index of 0.617. **Those two figures stay in one
  frame.** And **no table the reader can score themselves on** — the article shows what the
  stratification outputs, never its inputs and cut-points.
- **紅線 3 / 修正 23 (`bl-hematuria`, `bl-turbt`, `bl-report`, `bl-staging`)** — four hard bans:
  **no causal "delay → stage" sentence of any kind** (the effect is strongest in low-grade, Ta/Tis
  disease, which is the shape of confounding — quote AUA's own "have been suggested to contribute
  to"); **no "blue light reduces recurrence"** (PHOTO was negative; write it as unsettled); **no
  "any bladder tumour means a CT urogram"** (that is not EAU's recommendation — quote who it says
  should have one); and **WHO 1973 and WHO 2004/2022 grading are not interchangeable and no
  conversion table may be given** — the article asks which system the report used, never what the
  other one would say.
- **修正 3, 4, 5, 6 (`bl-report`, `bl-staging`)** — the denominators come from the **Taiwan cancer
  registry**, not EAU's unsourced "about 75%": long-form 112年 (diagnosis year 2023), 3,809 cases,
  **stage 0 + I = 72.8%, II–IV = 27.2%** (denominator 3,737, 72 unknown, AJCC 8th). Taiwan has
  **two** official counts — **2,562 (invasive only)** and **3,809 (long form, including 1,452 in
  situ)** — which may **never be interchanged or subtracted**, and every quotation says which.
  **SEER's "Localized" contains both T1 and T2**, so registry stage fields and the clinical line
  are two different languages and the articles say so. The bladder T definitions are **identical in
  TNM 8th and 9th**; Taiwan's registry still stages bladder with **AJCC 8th** and it may not be
  said that Taiwan has moved to the 9th.
- **紅線 7 / 修正 14, 15, 16, 17 (`bl-metastatic`, `bl-biomarker`)** — the fastest-ageing part of
  the topic; both articles carry the verification date **2026-09-13**.
  **修正 14 goes in `bl-metastatic`'s first paragraph**: Taiwan's NHI does **not** reimburse
  first-line enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab at all; clause **9.109** (effective 113/5/1)
  pays enfortumab vedotin only **third line, monotherapy, after platinum and after checkpoint-
  inhibitor failure, capped at six cycles per patient lifetime** — quoted verbatim as a translation
  of the clause. TFDA has licensed the combination (Padcev 衛部菌疫輸字第001212號, indication
  updated 2026-05-08). **The guideline and the licence changed; the payment did not**, which is why
  the title is "The first line after it has spread" and not "…has changed".
  **修正 15**: PD-L1 is a **three-way split** — the US and EU no longer use it to select first-line
  therapy, **but Taiwan's NHI still uses it as the payment gate** (pembrolizumab first line
  CPS≧10; nivolumab+GC TC≥1%; second line CPS≧10 / TC≧5%). **Never "PD-L1 no longer matters".**
  **修正 17**: EV-302's **2.5-year update** (OS 33.8 vs 15.9 months, HR 0.51) — say which analysis;
  the 2024 NEJM figures are not mixed in. ORR 67.7% / CR 29.1% is **immediately followed** by
  "a complete response is not a cure" and by **87% of peripheral neuropathy not fully resolved**.
  **修正 16**, three counter-facts, all mandatory: **sacituzumab govitecan's urothelial indication
  has been withdrawn**; **THOR cohort 2 was negative** (OS 10.9 vs 11.1 months, HR 1.18, 95% CI
  0.92–1.51, P=0.18); **erdafitinib has a Taiwan licence but zero hits in the reimbursement
  clauses**, and its indication has narrowed from FGFR2/3 to **FGFR3 only**. Those three are what
  make "a mutation means there is a drug" impossible to assemble.
- **紅線 8 / 修正 18, 19, 20 (`bl-utuc`)** — the topic's most distinctive article and the easiest
  to wreck in English.
  **"World's highest" is void**: it is a recurring narrative sentence in the literature, not a
  computed ranking, and **CI5 Volume XI does not include Taiwan**. The ceiling is **"three to four
  of every ten urothelial registrations in Taiwan are in the upper tract; the Western guidelines
  say one to two"**, and **all three caveats stay in the same passage**: ① the numerator (ICD-9
  189.1–189.9) includes urethra and other unspecified urinary organs; ② the denominator is
  invasive-only and excludes bladder carcinoma in situ; ③ CI5 XI has no Taiwan, so no same-basis
  ranking exists. **Never "the world's highest". Never "half of Taiwan's".** The Western 5–10% is
  itself moving (Norway 11.8%, the Netherlands 2.0 → 3.2 per 100,000) — write "the guideline says
  5 to 10%, and recent national registries are above one in ten".
  **The aristolochic acid material must make "taking Chinese medicine causes cancer"
  unconstructible.** Two ban announcements the **same day with different document numbers** —
  署授藥字第0920002350號 and 署授藥字第0920002349號, 2003-11-04 — **plus one management regulation
  that is NOT a ban**: 署授藥字第0930000756號 (2004-02-27), under which **細辛 was not banned**
  (root only, water decoction, HPLC control). That third notice is the evidence for the sentence
  that must be impossible to misread: **legally marketed Chinese medicine today is not the same
  thing as aristolochic-acid-containing medicine.** No manufacturer, clinic or practitioner is
  named. Document numbers are given in Arabic digits in English, as SPEC 修正 19 itself does.
  **Whether the ban brought incidence down is contested and both sides stand**: Sci Rep 2019
  reports −3.4% per year after 2011; J Cancer Res Clin Oncol 2023 reports a UTUC AAPC of **+1.47%**
  (2001–2018). **Both used the Taiwan Cancer Registry.** Never pick a side; never "the ban worked".
- **紅線 9 / 修正 21 (`bl-smoking`)** — **not one sentence may read as blame**, and English makes
  blame easier: second person, "you should", "failure to quit". The incidence evidence is strong
  and the post-diagnosis cessation evidence is **empty** — **SHR 0.99 (95% CI 0.61–1.61)**. Write
  "the evidence in this cell is empty", **not** "quitting does not help" and **not** "quitting
  stops it coming back". The two strengths are labelled and kept in separate sections.
- **紅線 10 / 修正 7's sister fact (`bl-nmibc-followup`, `bl-tmt-after`)** — EAU 2026's
  surveillance schedules are all **Weak**; only low risk has a five-year stopping point; **high and
  very-high risk is life-long, and so is upper-tract CT urography**. No "after N years you can
  stop" as a general rule. And the discomfort of cystoscopy is the real long-term burden of this
  disease — the English keeps that, rather than smoothing it into "regular check-ups".
- **修正 22 (`bl-daily`)** — ostomy supplies return **zero hits in both central subsidy schedules**:
  written as zero hits plus "ask the medical affairs office, your case manager, the social worker
  or the assistive-device centre", never inferred. What can be quoted is the one thing found:
  **disability assessment s610 / b620 grade 2 (moderate), "a permanent artificial bladder, passing
  urine through the abdominal wall for life"**. Several numbers were FAIL and stay absent — no
  clean-intermittent-self-catheterisation percentage, no B12 or acidosis rates, no appliance-change
  interval. **Müller 2023's 12.823 is also barred** (2026-09-14 ruling): the paper itself labels a
  linear-regression coefficient "odds ratio", and its interval is arithmetically symmetric — use the
  paper's unambiguous means instead, **69.8 against 55.1, p=0.004**. **Say where a number would be expected and is not available**, rather than inventing
  one.
- **Reimbursement register, everywhere**: 「查不到列項，問醫務課／個管師」 → "I could not find a
  listed item — ask the medical affairs office or your case manager". **Never "not covered" as a
  flat claim, and never "so you will have to pay for it yourself".** NHI clause quotations are
  translated faithfully and marked as translations of the clause. Zero-hit searches are reported as
  zero hits. **Media-reported prices are never cited**; self-pay amounts only from a dated official
  announcement.
- **Institutions**: study sources and official pages keep their institution names exactly where the
  Chinese has them; none is added; no evaluative adjective attaches to one. **No in-house
  configuration, no machine, no brand, no "our hospital has / does not have", and no criticism of
  any specialty.** Availability is "not every hospital does this — ask your own doctor where to be
  referred".
- **Statistics**: an odds ratio is not a multiple of probability; a sub-distribution hazard ratio is
  not either; non-inferiority proves "not much worse", not "as good as"; correlation is not
  causation. Guideline wording differences stand side by side, unreconciled — and **NICE gives no
  trimodality selection criteria, which is stated rather than filled in**.
- **Word-proportion rule**: 「約三分之二」= "about two-thirds", 「一成」= "10%", 「九成」= "nine in
  ten", 「三到四成」= "three to four in ten" — every number identical, every hedge at equal strength.
